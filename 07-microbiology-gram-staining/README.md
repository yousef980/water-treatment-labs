# Lab 07: Bacterial Identification via Gram Staining

## Objective
To master the differential Gram staining technique and characterize the morphology of lactic acid bacteria (LAB) found in yogurt samples.

## Principles
The Gram stain differentiates bacteria based on cell wall structure:
* **Gram-positive:** Thick peptidoglycan layer retains the primary violet stain.
* **Gram-negative:** Thin peptidoglycan layer and outer lipopolysaccharide membrane lose the violet stain and take up the counterstain (fuchsin).

## Results
Observation under $100\times$ oil immersion microscopy revealed:
* **Bacilli:** *Lactobacillus bulgaricus* (Gram+)
* **Cocci:** *Streptococcus thermophilus* (Gram+)

## Technical Significance
This is a prerequisite technique for environmental diagnostics, allowing for rapid categorization of bacterial strains found in water samples and soil matrices.
